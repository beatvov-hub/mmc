What is Mainichi Miru Inc.?

A company built around AI employees and human judgment.

Mainichi Miru Inc. is a fictional Japanese company operated as an ongoing creative project. Specialized AI employees take on distinct roles in research, planning, development, design, editing, operations, and AI literacy.

People set direction and make final decisions. The company records both the finished work and the conversations, tests, and observations that helped it take shape.

How it works

Different viewpoints, one shared practice.

01

Specialized AI employees

Each employee has a role and perspective, from research and implementation to editing and internal operations.

02

Human final decisions

AI employees investigate, propose, and build. A human director remains responsible for the final call.

03

Conversations become material

Useful ideas often begin as a casual exchange in the employee lounge, Bean & Bits.

04

Small experiments, public work

We test ideas before we publish them, then share the work when it has a reason to exist outside the company.
